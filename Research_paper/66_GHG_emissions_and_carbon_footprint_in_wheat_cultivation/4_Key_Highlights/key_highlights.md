## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Survey
Three districts, 2022–23; 12 tilled and 6 no-till practices; input × emission-factor method.

### 02. No-till lower
No-till practices had the lowest input emissions and positive CSI.

### 03. Fertiliser dominates
Fertiliser 74–83% of emissions; diesel second.

### 04. Footprint
About 0.50–0.82 kg CO₂e/kg grain; CP-1 highest, with the lowest yield.

### 05. Conflicting numbers
Emission range, CSI and carbon offset values differ between abstract, text and tables; 7 Table 4 totals don't add up.
