## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Human-induced
1.19 [1.0 to 1.4] °C for 2014–2023; 1.31 °C in 2023.

### 02. Rate
0.26 °C per decade, unprecedented in the instrumental record.

*(Reference: p. 2626)*

### 03. 2023 record
Observed 1.43 °C, partly internal variability (El Niño).

*(Reference: p. 2647)*

### 04. Emissions
53 ± 5.4 GtCO₂e/yr over 2013–2022; 55 in 2022.

### 05. Forcing
ERF 2.79 W m⁻² in 2023 vs 1750; weaker aerosol cooling.

*(Reference: p. 2633)*

### 06. Energy imbalance
0.96 W m⁻² over 2010–2023.

### 07. 1.5 °C (50%)
200 GtCO₂ from the start of 2024, down from 500 GtCO₂ in 2020.

### 08. 2 °C (50%)
1,100 GtCO₂ from the start of 2024.
