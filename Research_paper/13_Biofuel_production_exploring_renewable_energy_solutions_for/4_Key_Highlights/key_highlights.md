## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. First generation
Food crops; marginal GHG advantage and food competition.

*(Reference: p. 7)*

### 02. Second generation
Non-food residues such as straw; cellulosic ethanol can cut GHG up to 90% (cited).

*(Reference: pp. 7, 25)*

### 03. Third and fourth
Algae, and engineered microbes that turn CO₂ into fuel; still costly.

*(Reference: pp. 8–10)*

### 04. Anaerobic digestion
Gives methane-rich biogas plus digestate fertiliser.

*(Reference: p. 14)*

### 05. Blends
EU 5%, USA 10%, Brazil 27% ethanol.

*(Reference: p. 14)*

### 06. India
Led the Global Biofuel Alliance (September 2023).

*(Reference: p. 7)*

### 07. Rural economy
Jobs and extra income for farmers who supply feedstock.

*(Reference: p. 24)*

### 08. Risks
Land-use change, water use, biodiversity loss and feedstock supply.

*(Reference: pp. 23–25)*

### 09. Way forward
Policy support, R&D and integrated biorefineries.

*(Reference: p. 28)*
