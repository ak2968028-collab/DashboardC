## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Sustainable potential
Up to 0.92 Gt CO₂/yr; 0.43 with current practice, 1.29 at the theoretical maximum.

*(Reference: p. 4)*

### 02. Demand
China's NET demand in 2050–2060 is 0.01–2.91 Gt CO₂/yr (median 1.43).

*(Reference: pp. 1, 4)*

### 03. Net cost
About 90 $/t CO₂; 60–96 for residues, 101–144 for energy crops and grass.

*(Reference: p. 4)*

### 04. Other NETs
BECCS can exceed 100 and DACCS 200 $/t CO₂.

*(Reference: p. 1)*

### 05. Income
Syngas offsets 32–57% of costs; yield gains only 1–23% (yields +10% in China vs 35% globally).

*(Reference: p. 4)*

### 06. Pilot provinces
Guangxi, Henan and Shandong; start with agroforestry residues.

*(Reference: p. 6)*

### 07. Net benefit
Possible in some places where yields are high (cost map down to −52 $/t CO₂).

*(Reference: pp. 5, 7)*

### 08. Scope
Livestock manure excluded; Monte Carlo cost range −13 to 197 $/t CO₂.

*(Reference: p. 7)*
