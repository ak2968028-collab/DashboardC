## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Positive link
Diversity was linked to SOC (R² = 0.09) and soil C:N ratio.

*(Reference: p. 3)*

### 02. Climate decides
Strong only in the warmest, driest and most arid quartiles (R² 0.38–0.46).

*(Reference: p. 5)*

### 03. Not biomass
Diversity was not linked to biomass (P = 0.119).

*(Reference: p. 2)*

### 04. Quality
Diversity acts through organic matter quality (C:N), not quantity.

*(Reference: p. 7)*

### 05. Drivers
SOC fell with temperature and rose with the aridity index; clay had no effect.

*(Reference: p. 6)*

### 06. Restore diversity
Likely enhances SOC sequestration, mostly in warm, arid climates.

*(Reference: p. 1)*
