## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Three credit types
Removed (tree planting), avoided (not cutting trees) and reduced (efficient technology) emissions.

### 02. Current schemes
China CCER, EU ETS, California GWSA; aimed at transport, steel, cement.

### 03. Cost barrier
Credit income could offset tree establishment and management costs.

### 04. India
National Agroforestry Policy 2014 lists carbon credit schemes for agroforestry as a pathway.

### 05. Verification
Weak monitoring and verification, double counting and permanence.
