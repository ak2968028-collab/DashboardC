## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Ground data
1,188,771 GFBI inventory plots (1,089,026 after checks) plus three satellite biomass maps.

### 02. Agreement
Ground and satellite potentials differ by only 12% globally; existing tree carbon 367 vs 394 Gt C.

*(Reference: pp. 92, 94–95)*

### 03. Living trees
600 Gt C potential on 4.4 billion ha; 217 Gt C above current stocks, 123 of it tropical.

*(Reference: p. 95)*

### 04. All pools
328 Gt C (221–472) below natural potential; 102 Gt C on urban, crop and pasture land.

*(Reference: pp. 96, 98)*

### 05. Realistic share
226 Gt C: 61% (139) in existing forests, 39% (87) in removed or fragmented areas.

*(Reference: pp. 92, 96)*

### 06. Grazing
About 41% of the gap outside forests is on pasture and rangeland.

*(Reference: p. 97)*

### 07. Deforestation
Releases 0.9–2.3 Gt C/yr, about 15% of human emissions.

*(Reference: p. 92)*

### 08. Caveats
Biophysical only; 1979–2013 climate; needs natural species diversity.

*(Reference: pp. 98–99)*

### 09. People
Restoration led by local communities; agroforestry named as a livelihood option.

*(Reference: pp. 97, 99)*
