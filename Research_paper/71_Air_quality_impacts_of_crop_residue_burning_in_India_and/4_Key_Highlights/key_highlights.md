## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Deaths and cost
44,000–98,000 deaths and about 23 bn USD a year, 2003–2019.

### 02. Sources
Punjab 64%, Haryana 11%, UP 5.7% of fire-related exposure; rice-season burning 64%.

### 03. Districts
Six Punjab districts cause 40% of impacts (27,000 deaths, 9.0 bn USD).

### 04. Timing
Burning 2 h earlier in November in Punjab: about 9,600 deaths averted a year (3.2 bn USD).

### 05. Not GHG
Primary PM₂.₅ health impacts only; no greenhouse gas estimates.
