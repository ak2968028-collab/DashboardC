## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Steady for three decades
3.59, 3.57 and 3.53 Pg C/yr in the 1990s, 2000s and 2010s; statistically stable.

### 02. Ground-based coverage
Inventories for about 4.0 billion ha, 95% of global forest.

### 03. Net of deforestation
Net forest sink 0.93, 1.66 and 1.39 Pg C/yr; no significant trend.

### 04. Gains
Temperate +30% (China's plantings) and tropical regrowth +29% (more regrowth area).

### 05. Losses
Boreal −36% (fire, insects, logging) and tropical intact −31% (loss of 26% of intact area).

### 06. Regrowth works fast
Tropical regrowth sequesters carbon about 5 times faster than intact forest.

### 07. Where carbon sits
870 Pg C: soil 45%, living biomass 43%, dead wood 8%, litter 4%.

### 08. Wood products store little
Only ~10% of harvested carbon is stored in products; they add 6% of the sink.

### 09. Half of fossil emissions
107 Pg C since 1990 = 46% of fossil emissions, but 63% of this was negated by tropical deforestation.

### 10. Models may be too optimistic
Models show rising forest uptake; inventories show it flat.

### 11. Stop deforestation first
Then restore degraded land, reforest, manage climate-smart and favour long-lived wood products.
