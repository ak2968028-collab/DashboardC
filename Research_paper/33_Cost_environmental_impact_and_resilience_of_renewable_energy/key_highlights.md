## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Solar PV
LCOE fell from $0.417 to $0.048 per kWh (2010–2021), −88%; installed cost −82%.

### 02. Other sources
Onshore wind −68%, offshore wind −60%, CSP −68%, bioenergy −14%; hydro +24%, geothermal +34%.

### 03. Wind and hydro exposed
Up to −40% wind in northern North America; up to −41% hydropower in Portugal by 2050.

*(Reference: pp. 750–752)*

### 04. Solar most resilient
Solar appears the least impacted energy source.

*(Reference: p. 751)*

### 05. Biomass sensitive
+1 °C cuts wheat yield 6.0%, maize 7.4%, rice 3.2%.

*(Reference: p. 751)*

### 06. Ranking
Hydropower most damaging; solar PV needs careful installation; wind and biomass negligible impact.

### 07. Indian village warning
In a remote block of Uttaranchal, emissions rose after renewables were installed.

### 08. Money flows
$5.9 trillion fossil subsidies (2020); $4 trillion/yr renewable investment needed by 2030.

*(Reference: p. 756)*

### 09. Outlook
Renewables could give 65% of world electricity by 2030 and decarbonise 90% of power by 2050.

*(Reference: p. 745)*
