## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Total
3.58 Gt C (13.12 Gt CO₂e) per year allocated to mycorrhizal mycelium.

*(Reference: p. R565)*

### 02. By type
EcM 9.07, AM 3.93, ErM 0.12 Gt CO₂e per year.

*(Reference: p. R565)*

### 03. Share of NPP
EcM 13.1%, AM 6.2%, ErM 3.5%; up to 50% in some experiments.

*(Reference: p. R564)*

### 04. Crops
Crops 3.3%, four times less than wild plants; wheat, barley, maize < 0.1%.

*(Reference: p. R565)*

### 05. Soil carbon
Hyphae, necromass and exudates stabilise soil carbon; respiration returns some.

*(Reference: pp. R566–R568)*

### 06. Caveat
Residence time unknown; no data from Africa, Oceania or SE Asia.

*(Reference: pp. R564, R569)*
