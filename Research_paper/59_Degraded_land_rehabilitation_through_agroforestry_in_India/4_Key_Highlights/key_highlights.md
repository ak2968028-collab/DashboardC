## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Mostly erosion
120.7 Mha degraded; water (73.3 Mha) and wind (12.4 Mha) erosion on arable land make up about 71%.

### 02. Drivers
2.07 Mha tree cover lost (2001–2021), grazing on 5.65 Mha, shifting cultivation on 7.6 Mha.

*(Reference: p. 2)*

### 03. Wide range by zone
Table 4 runs from 1.55 to 20–25 Mg C/ha/yr; Middle Gangetic Plains (teak + sorghum/groundnut) 2.32.

### 04. Abstract range is misleading
'1.80 to 3.50' are the first and last rows of Table 4, not its minimum and maximum.

*(Reference: pp. 1, 8)*

### 05. Slopes and ravines
Hedgerows cut soil loss 94% and runoff 78% (NE India); Jhansi silvipasture lost only 0.9 Mg/ha of soil.

### 06. Sodic and saline soils
Acacia nilotica, Prosopis + Kallar grass, Eucalyptus bio-drainage (water table −0.75 m in 5 years).

### 07. Business models and policy
Supply chains, buy-back with MSP, insurance and credit to scale agroforestry; National Agroforestry Policy support.

*(Reference: pp. 20–21)*
