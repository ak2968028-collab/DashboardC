## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Biggest cut
Not burning: 618 (Bihar) and 903 (Punjab) kg CO₂e/ha.

### 02. Zero tillage
265 kg CO₂e/ha (Punjab; assumed for Bihar).

### 03. Eligible
Zero-till wheat (both states) and less N (Punjab).

*(Reference: p. 1)*

### 04. Ineligible
No burning in Punjab: banned and > 34% of area unburned.

*(Reference: p. 5)*
