## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Sources
Fossil 10.1 ± 0.5 + land use 1.0 ± 0.7 = 11.1 ± 0.9 GtC (40.6 GtCO₂).

*(Reference: p. 968)*

### 02. Sinks
Atmosphere 5.9, ocean 2.9, land 2.3 ± 1.0 GtC: the lowest land sink since 2015.

*(Reference: pp. 968–969)*

### 03. India
+3.6%/yr over 2014–2023; 8% of world fossil CO₂ in 2023; 0.6 tC per person.

*(Reference: p. 985)*

### 04. Cumulative fossil CO₂
1850–2023: USA 120 GtC (24%), EU 80 (16%), China 75 (15%), India 15 (3%).

*(Reference: p. 985)*

### 05. Years left
From 2025: 65 GtC for 1.5 °C, 160 for 1.7 °C, 305 for 2 °C (≈ 6, 14, 27 years).

*(Reference: p. 1011)*

### 06. Deforestation
About 1.7 GtC/yr emitted; regrowth and planting take up 1.2 GtC/yr (two-thirds).

*(Reference: p. 969)*

### 07. Three gaps
Land-use change emissions, northern extra-tropical land flux and the mean ocean sink.
