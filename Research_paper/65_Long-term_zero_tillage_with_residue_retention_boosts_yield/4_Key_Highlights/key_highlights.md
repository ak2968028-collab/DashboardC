## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. GWP
ZTDSR-THSW + R 5,377 vs PTR-CTW 11,688 kg CO₂e/ha/yr; the cut comes mainly from dropping puddled rice.

### 02. Energy
47,110 vs 65,830 MJ/ha; EUE 13.6 vs 9.9; system yield comparable (10,744 vs 11,083 kg/ha).

### 03. Modelled emissions
GHG from emission factors; CH₄ assumed negligible in DSR.

### 04. Carbon indices
11.43 is the CSI, not the CER (13.43), and neither matches the stated formulas.
