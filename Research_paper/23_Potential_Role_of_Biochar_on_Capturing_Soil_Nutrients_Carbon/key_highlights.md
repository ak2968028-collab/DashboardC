## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Process decides
Feedstock, residence time and pyrolysis conditions control biochar properties.

*(Reference: p. 1)*

### 02. Temperature
High pyrolysis heat may reduce nutrient availability to plants.

*(Reference: pp. 1, 3)*

### 03. Nutrients
Biochars release nutrients at different rates; add N fertiliser.

*(Reference: p. 6)*

### 04. Stability
Wood biochar is more stable than crop-residue biochar.

*(Reference: p. 7)*

### 05. GHG
Scaling up could cut net human GHG by up to 12% (cited).

*(Reference: p. 10)*

### 06. Drawbacks
Over-use raised weeds ~200%; effects are soil- and crop-specific.

*(Reference: p. 9)*
