## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Headline value
$185 per tCO₂ (5–95%: $44–$413, 2020 US$) at a 2% near-term discount rate.

### 02. Sensitivity to discounting
Means of $80 (3%), $118 (2.5%) and $308 (1.5%).

*(Reference: pp. 689–690)*

### 03. Compared with policy today
3.6 times the US government's $51 per tCO₂ (3% rate).

*(Reference: p. 689)*

### 04. GIVE model
Probabilistic socioeconomics (RFF-SPs), FaIR climate, BRICK sea level, sectoral damages, growth-linked discounting.

*(Reference: pp. 688–689)*

### 05. Versus DICE
DICE-2016R gives $44; the 2% discount rate adds $105 (74% of the change).

### 06. Mortality and farming dominate
Temperature mortality $90 and agriculture $84; energy $9; sea-level rise $2.

### 07. Agriculture is most uncertain
Range −$23 to $263: CO₂ fertilisation could bring gains.

*(Reference: p. 690)*

### 08. Probably conservative
Biodiversity, labour, conflict, migration and most tipping points are not counted.

*(Reference: pp. 690–691)*

### 09. Use a range
Results depend strongly on the discount rate; values are global, in US dollars.

*(Reference: pp. 689–690)*
