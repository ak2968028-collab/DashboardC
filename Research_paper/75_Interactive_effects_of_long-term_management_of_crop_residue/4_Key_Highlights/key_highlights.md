## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Both straws
Yield without P (6.10 t/ha) on par with 30 and 60 kg P₂O₅/ha.

### 02. Rice straw only
30 kg P₂O₅/ha matched 60 kg (T3, T4).

### 03. Structure
T5: bulk density 1.34 vs 1.42 g/cm³; infiltration 2.2 vs 1.4 cm/h (T2).

### 04. Biology
Fungi 53.2 vs 19.8 × 10³ CFU/g under burning.
