## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Area-scaled
7,870 kg CO₂e/ha/yr, about 94% from CH₄.

### 02. Latitude
CH₄ 614 kg/ha/yr at 10°S–10°N vs 272 above 40°N.

### 03. India
11–19% of global rice CH₄ and 17% of rice N₂O.

### 04. Drivers
Organic matter and continuous flooding drive CH₄; the N rate drives N₂O.

### 05. Climate
Elevated CO₂ +4–40% CH₄; warming +15–23% CH₄.

### 06. Trend
Yield-scaled CH₄ down 38–55% since ~1980 (higher yields, more drainage).

### 07. Headline cuts
New varieties –24%, non-continuous flooding –44%, straw removal –46% GHG.

### 08. Drainage
–53% CH₄ in the rice season; N₂O rises but rarely cancels the gain.

### 09. Straw trade-off
Extra CH₄ GWP was 3.2–3.9× the SOC gain from straw.

### 10. Biochar
–22% N₂O in experiments of 2 years or more; –13% CH₄.

### 11. Data
More measurements needed in low latitudes, including southern India.
