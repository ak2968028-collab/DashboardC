## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Renewables
A higher renewable share goes with lower CO₂ and GHG emissions.

### 02. Direction
Energy mix Granger-causes emissions; not the reverse.

### 03. Energy mix
Renewables rose from about 10% (1990) to nearly 30% (2021).

*(Reference: p. 11)*

### 04. Air pollution
Measured only as CO₂ and GHG emissions; no air-quality data.
