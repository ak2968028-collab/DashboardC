## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Not passive pipes
Rivers metabolise organic carbon, emit CO₂ and CH₄, and bury carbon in sediments, floodplains and deltas.

### 02. Strong heterotrophy
NEP: tropical –606, temperate –260, high-latitude –438 g C/m²/yr; rivers are among Earth's most heterotrophic ecosystems.

### 03. Land to rivers
0.72 Pg C/yr organic + 2.44 Pg C/yr inorganic = 3.2 Pg C/yr, about a quarter of terrestrial NEP (11 Pg C/yr).

### 04. Respired in rivers
River NEP –0.27 Pg C/yr: about 37% of incoming organic carbon becomes CO₂.

### 05. Reaches the coast
Only about 26% of land carbon delivered to rivers reaches the coastal ocean.

### 06. River CO₂
1.9–2.3 Pg C/yr, over 70% from the tropics; small steep streams emit disproportionately.

### 07. River CH₄
About 0.005 Pg C/yr (mean), 0.008 (median).

### 08. Reservoirs
Emit 0.037 (UB 0.3) and bury 0.027 (UB 0.18) Pg C/yr; deep anoxic water favours methane.

### 09. Farmland and dams
Agricultural soils supply OC and nutrients that alter river metabolism; dams change metabolism downstream.

### 10. River Observing System
Authors call for RIOS: sensors, satellites, scaling laws and models, with more tropical data.
