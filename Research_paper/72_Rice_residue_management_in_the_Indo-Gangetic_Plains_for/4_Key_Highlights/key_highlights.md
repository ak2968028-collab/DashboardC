## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Scale
About 50 Mt of rice straw burned a year, nearly half in north-west India.

### 02. Emissions
About 1.5 Mt particulates and 150 Mt CO₂ a year.

### 03. Happy Seeder
>90% of surveyed Punjab farmers say it saves water, labour and fuel; only 67.1% see short-term yield gains.

*(Reference: p. 13)*

### 04. Barriers
25-day window, wet straw, low capacity, even with a 50% subsidy.

*(Reference: p. 13)*

### 05. Biomass
UP is the largest producer of kharif (19.1%) and rabi (17%) biomass.

*(Reference: p. 3)*
