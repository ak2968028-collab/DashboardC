## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Water
Punjab groundwater fell 8.91 m (2000–2019); 80% of blocks over-exploited.

*(Reference: p. 5)*

### 02. Burning
40% (11.3 Mt) of Punjab and Haryana paddy straw is burned; Punjab 88% of it.

*(Reference: p. 4)*

### 03. Savings
11–18% water, 11–66% labour; cost USD 448 vs 555/ha with zero-till wheat.

*(Reference: pp. 7–8)*

### 04. Methane
0.1–0.3 vs 0.8–1.9 t CO₂e/ha; total GWP 1.94 vs 2.91 t/ha (Punjab).

*(Reference: p. 9)*

### 05. Fire trend
UP fire events fell 24% in 2018 vs 2017.

*(Reference: p. 7)*
