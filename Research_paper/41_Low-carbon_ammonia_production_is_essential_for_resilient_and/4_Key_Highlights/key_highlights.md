## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Fertiliser chain
1,100–1,300 Mt CO₂e/yr from production, use and transport.

*(Reference: p. 610)*

### 02. Per tonne
2.6–2.9 t CO₂ per t NH₃ on average; 3.2–4.5 t from coal.

*(Reference: p. 612)*

### 03. Demand vs capacity
18% of world N demand but 8% of capacity; net importer.

*(Reference: p. 612)*

### 04. CCS
~70% lower emissions, still gas-dependent.

*(Reference: p. 615)*

### 05. Electrolysis and biomass
2–3× the cost; 100–300× the land and water.

*(Reference: p. 610)*
