# Key Highlights: The carbon costs of global wood harvests

**Authors:** Liqing Peng, Timothy D. Searchinger, Jessica Zionts, Richard Waite
**Journal / Year:** Nature, vol. 620, pp. 110–115 (3 August 2023) (2022)
**Official DOI:** [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06187-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06187-1)

## Core Statistics & Results

| Metric | Finding / Significance |
| --- | --- |
| **3.5–4.2 Gt** | CO₂e/yr harvest carbon cost |
| **+54%** | harvest by 2050 |
| **~50%** | of costs from wood fuel |
| **−3%** | if 100-yr horizon |

## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Annual cost
3.5–4.2 Gt CO₂e/yr, like agricultural expansion (3–4).

*(Reference: p. 114)*

### 02. Demand
Harvest rises 3.7 to 5.7 billion m³ by 2050.

*(Reference: p. 112)*

### 03. No netting
Growth that would happen anyway cannot offset new harvests.

*(Reference: p. 111)*

### 04. Robust
Discount rate and horizon barely change the result.

*(Reference: p. 112)*

### 05. Less wood fuel
Halving wood fuel: 3.6 Gt CO₂e/yr and 70 Mha less harvest.

*(Reference: pp. 112, 114)*
