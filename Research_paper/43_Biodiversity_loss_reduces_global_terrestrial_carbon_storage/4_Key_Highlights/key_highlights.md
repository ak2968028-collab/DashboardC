## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Global loss
Plant species loss could release 7.44–145.95 Pg C over the long term.

*(Reference: p. 1)*

### 02. Rivals other sources
12–271% of land-use emissions expected by 2100.

*(Reference: pp. 4–5)*

### 03. Tropics
Amazon, central Africa and Southeast Asia lose the most carbon.

*(Reference: p. 3)*

### 04. Diversity, not just area
Restore diverse native vegetation; avoid non-native monocultures.

*(Reference: p. 6)*

### 05. Feedback loop
More warming, more species loss, more emissions.

*(Reference: p. 1)*
