## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Biomass gain
+13.6 ± 1.5 Pg C (2020–2100), stock 21.6–24.3 Pg C in 2100.

*(Reference: p. 3)*

### 02. Survival
Raising new-planting survival from 47% to 85% adds 1.0–1.1 Pg C.

*(Reference: p. 3)*

### 03. Where
Natural non-timber forests 57.7% of the gain; timber forests only 8.1%.

*(Reference: p. 4, Fig. 2)*

### 04. Timing
Ignoring harvest effects on forest age puts the sink peak 1–3 decades too early.

*(Reference: pp. 1, 5)*

### 05. Wood pool
1.9 ± 0.1 Pg C in 2100; 61.5% short-lived paper limits storage.

*(Reference: p. 5)*

### 06. Act now
Optimal management adds 2.3 ± 0.03 Pg C; each year of delay loses 11–17 Tg C.

*(Reference: p. 5, Table 1)*

### 07. Check
Table 1 practices (4.5–6.7 Tg C/yr) do not add up to the 28.1 Tg C/yr in the text.

*(Reference: p. 5 vs Table 1, p. 7)*
