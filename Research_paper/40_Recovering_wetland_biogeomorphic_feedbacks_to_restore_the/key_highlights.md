# Key Highlights: Recovering wetland biogeomorphic feedbacks to restore the world’s biotic carbon hotspots

**Authors:** Ralph J. M. Temmink (Utrecht University; NIOZ; Radboud University), Leon P. M. Lamers, Christine Angelini, Tjeerd J. Bouma, Christian Fritz, Johan van de Koppel, Robin Lexmond, Max Rietkerk, Brian R. Silliman, Hans Joosten, Tjisse van der Heide (NIOZ)

**Official DOI:** [https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abn1479](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abn1479)

## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Share
1% of Earth's surface, 20% of ecosystem organic carbon.

*(Reference: p. 4)*

### 02. Density
Peatlands 1000–2000 Mg C/ha vs forests 150–230.

*(Reference: p. 9)*

### 03. Rate
About 1% a year, releasing 0.5 Pg C/yr (5% of human emissions).

*(Reference: p. 13)*

### 04. Duration
Drained peat keeps emitting for a century or more.

*(Reference: p. 13)*

### 05. Feedbacks
Restart vegetation–landform feedbacks; clumped planting doubled yields.

*(Reference: p. 14)*
