## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Concentration
270 ppb (1750) to 336 ppb (2022), highest in 800,000 years; growth >1.3 ppb/yr in 2020–2021.

*(Reference: pp. 2544–2545)*

### 02. Scenarios
Observed levels exceed all CMIP6 projections.

*(Reference: p. 2545)*

### 03. Human total
Up 40% (4.8 to 6.7 Tg N/yr) from 1980 to 2020.

*(Reference: p. 2545)*

### 04. Sources 2020
Direct agriculture 3.9, other direct 2.1, indirect 1.3 Tg N/yr.

*(Reference: p. 2545; Table 3)*

### 05. Budget 2020
18.5 (BU) and 17.0 (TD) Tg N/yr in total.

*(Reference: p. 2545)*

### 06. South Asia
+92%; direct agricultural N₂O 0.19 to 0.55 Tg N/yr from more fertiliser.

*(Reference: pp. 2546, 2572)*

### 07. Shifts
Europe –31%; China +82% and top emitter since the 2010s.

*(Reference: p. 2546)*

### 08. Northern India
High fertiliser use and large soil N₂O uncertainty.

*(Reference: p. 2546)*
