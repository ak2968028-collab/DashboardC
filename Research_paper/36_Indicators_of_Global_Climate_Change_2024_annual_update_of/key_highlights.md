## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Human-induced
1.22 [1.0 to 1.5] °C for 2015–2024; 1.36 °C in 2024.

### 02. Rate
0.27 °C per decade, unprecedented.

### 03. 2024
Observed 1.52 °C, a typical year given El Niño.

*(Reference: p. 2642)*

### 04. Emissions
53.6 ± 5.2 GtCO₂e/yr over 2014–2023; 55.4 in 2023.

### 05. Forcing
ERF 2.97 W m⁻² in 2024 vs 1750.

*(Reference: p. 2651)*

### 06. Energy imbalance
0.99 W m⁻² over 2012–2024, 25% above AR6.

### 07. 1.5 °C (50%)
160 GtCO₂ from 2025 after the 2026 corrigendum (paper: 130).

### 08. 2 °C (50%)
1,080 GtCO₂ from 2025.
