## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Two IGP sites
Karnal (Haryana) and Samastipur (Bihar), both started in 2012; soil sampled after 3 and 5 years.

*(Reference: p. 3, p. 6)*

### 02. Practices
Zero tillage, permanent raised beds, residue retention and mungbean vs conventional puddled rice-wheat.

### 03. Higher system yields
K6 (raised-bed maize-wheat-mungbean) 14.3 Mg/ha, +20.2% over K1; S2 15.0 Mg/ha, +23.0% over S1.

### 04. Gains in the top 5 cm
Karnal 0–5 cm TOC +11–20% in K4/K6; 0–15 cm +8–13%. Samastipur 5–15 cm unchanged.

*(Reference: p. 9)*

### 05. Labile carbon responds first
POXC stock +37.0% (K4, 2017) and +93.8% (S2, 2018); cPOM carbon in K4 3.5× K1 at 0–15 cm.

*(Reference: pp. 9–10)*

### 06. Early indicators
POXC, POC and cPOM proposed for tracking short-term sequestration under CA.

### 07. 3-year results
The abstract reports no 3-year change at Karnal, but the Results show significant 0–5 cm TOC gains in 2015.

*(Reference: pp. 1, 9)*
