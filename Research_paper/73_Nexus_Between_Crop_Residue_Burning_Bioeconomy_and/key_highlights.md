**Journal / Year:** Frontiers in Energy Research, vol. 8, article 614212 (2020)
**Official DOI:** [https://doi.org/10.3389/fenrg.2020.614212](https://doi.org/10.3389/fenrg.2020.614212)

## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Emissions
176.1 Tg CO₂, 313.9 Gg CH₄, 8.14 Gg N₂O, 453.4 Gg PM₂.₅ in 2017–18.

### 02. Crops
Wheat, rice and sugarcane give about 80% of residue burned.

### 03. Formula
R = P × N × D × B × F; rice N 1.5, D 0.86; B 0.25 and F 0.9 for all crops.

### 04. Caution
Burned totals depend on the 25% default, not observed fire data.

### 05. Bioeconomy
Smart farming, waste bioeconomy, capacity building and policy.
