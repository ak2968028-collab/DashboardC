## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Extra carbon
Protection preserved 9.65 Gt C, about one year of fossil emissions.

*(Reference: p. 1)*

### 02. Brazil
3.54 Gt, 36.6% of the global signal.

*(Reference: p. 2)*

### 03. Avoided loss
Mainly avoided deforestation; lidar also reveals hidden degradation.

*(Reference: p. 2)*

### 04. Coverage
52°N–52°S, aboveground woody carbon only.

*(Reference: pp. 2, 6)*

### 05. Mangroves
Near-zero effect; GEDI misses short stands.

*(Reference: p. 4)*
