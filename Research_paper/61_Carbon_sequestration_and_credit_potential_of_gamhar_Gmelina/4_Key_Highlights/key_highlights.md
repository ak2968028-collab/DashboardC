## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Ranchi, 2016–2018
7 treatments × 5 replications; gamhar at 8 × 2.5 m (500 trees/ha) with arhar, cowpea-mustard or greengram-mustard.

*(Reference: pp. 9–10)*

### 02. Young trees store little yet
Sole gamhar held only 0.84 t C/ha; sole greengram-mustard 7.33 and gamhar + arhar 6.68 (pooled).

### 03. Soil carbon
Gamhar + arhar had 348.5% more soil carbon than sole gamhar (pooled).

*(Reference: p. 4, Table 6)*

### 04. Tree biomass shares
Stem 50–60%, leaves 19–20%, branches 10.7–12.7%; roots ~20% (assumed 25% of above-ground).

### 05. Credit value
Up to 26.9 credits/ha (US$538) for sole greengram-mustard; 24.5 (US$490) for gamhar + arhar.

### 06. Not significant for trees
Intercropping raised gamhar biomass slightly, but not significantly.

*(Reference: p. 4, Tables 2–3)*
