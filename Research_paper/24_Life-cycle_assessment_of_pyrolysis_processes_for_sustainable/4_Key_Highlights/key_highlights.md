## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Rice straw
1.14 / 1.64 / 1.10 t CO₂-eq per t (fast / intermediate / slow).

### 02. End use
Soil use −386 to −933 vs energy use −240 to −787 kg CO₂-eq/t.

### 03. Moisture
Wet feedstock gives marginal benefit; dry straw first.

### 04. Scale
Brick kiln −229 vs large plant −360 kg CO₂-eq/t rice husk.

### 05. Transport
Long hauls can cancel the gains of central plants.

### 06. Boundaries
Different functional units and boundaries limit comparison.

### 07. Circular economy
Combine LCA with circular-economy and cost analysis.
