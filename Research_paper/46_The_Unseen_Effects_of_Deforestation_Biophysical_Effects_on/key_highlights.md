## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Net warming
Clearing forest warms the planet up to 50°N; beyond, albedo cooling dominates.

*(Reference: p. 6)*

### 02. Tropics
Biophysical warming adds 35–60% to CO₂ warming.

*(Reference: p. 6)*

### 03. Heat extremes
Forests cut hot-day and hot-season temperatures at all latitudes.

*(Reference: p. 7)*

### 04. Near 25°N
Annual biophysical effect close to zero; carbon effect still cools.

*(Reference: p. 6)*

### 05. Beyond carbon
Metrics should count biophysical effects.

*(Reference: p. 10)*
