## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Smaller land sink
Net land flux down 31% to 1.4 GtC/yr; ocean sink up 8%.

*(Reference: p. 8)*

### 02. Balanced
Imbalance −0.1 GtC/yr; trend no longer significant.

*(Reference: p. 9)*

### 03. Weaker sinks
Land sink −23%, ocean −6%: +8.3 ppm CO₂ since 1960.

*(Reference: p. 9)*

### 04. Tropics
SE Asia (deforestation) and South America (climate) turning to sources.

*(Reference: p. 10)*

### 05. Latest figures
423 ppm CO₂ in 2024; 1.36 °C human-induced warming.

*(Reference: p. 3)*
