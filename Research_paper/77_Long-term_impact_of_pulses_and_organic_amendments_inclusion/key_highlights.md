## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Rotation
Pulse rotations: SOC +10–18% over maize–wheat after 14 years.

### 02. INM
FYM + residue with fertiliser: SOC +11–18% over fertiliser alone.

*(Reference: p. 6)*

### 03. Physical
Porosity +7–9%; water holding +10% (P–W).

### 04. Yield
Mungbean: maize +26%, wheat +21% (2015–16).

### 05. Abstract %
25–50% SOC gains are plot-specific vs M–W (RDF).

*(Reference: p. 1)*
