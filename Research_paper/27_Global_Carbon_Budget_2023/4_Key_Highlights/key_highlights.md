## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Sources
Fossil 9.9 ± 0.5 + land use 1.2 ± 0.7 = 11.1 GtC (40.7 GtCO₂).

*(Reference: p. 5304)*

### 02. Sinks
Atmosphere 4.6, ocean 2.8, land 3.8 ± 0.8 GtC; imbalance −0.1 GtC.

*(Reference: p. 5304)*

### 03. India
+3.5%/yr over 2013–2022; 8% of world fossil CO₂ in 2022; 0.5 tC per person.

*(Reference: pp. 5319–5320)*

### 04. Cumulative fossil CO₂
1850–2022: USA 115 GtC (24%), EU 80 (17%), China 70 (15%), India 15 (3%).

*(Reference: p. 5319)*

### 05. Years left
From 2024: 75 GtC for 1.5 °C, 175 for 1.7 °C, 315 for 2 °C (≈ 7, 15, 28 years).

*(Reference: p. 5345)*

### 06. Deforestation
About 1.9 GtC/yr emitted; re-/afforestation and forestry take up 1.3 GtC/yr.

*(Reference: p. 5304)*

### 07. Three gaps
Land-use change emissions, northern extra-tropical land flux and the recent ocean sink trend.
