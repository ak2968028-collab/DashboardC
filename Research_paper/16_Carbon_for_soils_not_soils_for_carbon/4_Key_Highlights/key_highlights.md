**Journal / Year:** Global Change Biology, vol. 29, issue 9, pp. 2384–2398 (2023) (2023)

## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Saturation
Including it cuts the 2100 contribution by 53–81%; almost no estimates did.

*(Reference: pp. 1, 4)*

### 02. Estimates
Land-constrained 3.3 and input-based 0.52 Gt CO₂/yr; historical losses 40–537 Gt C.

*(Reference: p. 3)*

### 03. Permanence
Gains are reversible and cannot replace emission cuts.

*(Reference: p. 4)*

### 04. Yields
Effects are negative, neutral or positive; often neutral once NPK is corrected.

*(Reference: pp. 1, 9)*

### 05. Context
Larger benefits on sandy soils; gain levels off near 2% SOC.

*(Reference: p. 9)*

### 06. Stocks
Many studies report concentration, not stock, and inconsistent depths.

*(Reference: p. 3)*

### 07. Policy
No global SOC targets for farm soils; soil-smart, local agriculture.

*(Reference: p. 1)*

### 08. Co-benefit
Treat SOC gains as a co-benefit, not the main goal.

*(Reference: p. 1)*
