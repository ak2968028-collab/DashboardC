## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Practices
Necromass C: N +12%, cover crops +14%, NT/RT +20%, manure +21%, straw +21%.

### 02. Biochar
No effect on necromass; raised SOC directly.

### 03. Microbes
Straw and NT/RT favour fungal necromass; manure favours bacterial.

### 04. Climate
Straw best in semi-arid and cool climates; NT/RT in warm, humid areas; manure everywhere (+42% warm).

### 05. Soil
Straw needs pH < 8; no response in sandy soils; larger gains when SOC > 12 g/kg.

### 06. Time
No effect in trials ≤3 years except manure.

### 07. Efficiency
Necromass is 45–52% of the SOC gain, 9–16% more than under N alone.
