## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Balance
Ration balancing works on farms for buffalo and cattle.

*(Reference: p. 159)*

### 02. Forage
Tender grass and concentrates beat mature, fibrous feed.

*(Reference: pp. 159–160)*

### 03. Herbal
Harit Dhara up to 17–20%, at Rs 6/kg.

*(Reference: p. 162)*

### 04. Avoid
Antibiotics and ionophores not recommended.

*(Reference: p. 160)*

### 05. Green fodder
Year-round greens cut residue-heavy diets.

*(Reference: p. 157)*
