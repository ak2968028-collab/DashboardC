## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Paired trials
Haryana 1,094, Punjab 245, Bihar 251; 730 rice and 864 wheat; 2013–14 to 2016–17.

### 02. GHG cut
GWP about 2.5% lower in rice and 12–20% lower in wheat than farmer practice (modelled).

### 03. Yield
NE out-yielded FFP in all 135 East IGP rice pairs; yields rose about 4–12%.

### 04. Cost
More K₂O raised fertiliser cost for 55% of rice and 51% of wheat farmers.

### 05. Upper-bound totals
13.9 Mt more grain, 1.44 Mt less N and 5.24 Mt CO₂e less per year (abstract says 5.34; parts add to 5.24).
