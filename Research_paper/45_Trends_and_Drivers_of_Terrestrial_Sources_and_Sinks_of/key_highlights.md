## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Net sink
CO₂ +3.8, climate −0.58, land use −1.6: net 1.7 Pg C/yr (2012–2021).

*(Reference: p. 1)*

### 02. Where
Largest net uptake outside the tropics.

*(Reference: p. 2)*

### 03. South Asia
Small net sink of 0.06 Pg C/yr; land use −0.09.

*(Reference: p. 19)*

### 04. Forests and drylands
Equal shares of mean and trend; drylands drive yearly swings.

*(Reference: p. 1)*

### 05. Caution
Land-use emissions include ~40% lost sink capacity.

*(Reference: p. 10)*
