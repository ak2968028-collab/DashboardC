## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Subsidy
Urea subsidy USD 228.5/t (2010–22) drives N overuse.

*(Reference: p. 3)*

### 02. NBS 2010
P use fell from 40.6 to 28.0 kg P₂O₅/ha.

*(Reference: p. 7)*

### 03. Surplus N
17.5 → 100.8 kg N/ha (1961–2021).

*(Reference: p. 8)*

### 04. Neem urea
No clear effect on yields, urea rates or N₂O.

*(Reference: p. 8)*

### 05. Rebalance
