## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Three controls
Moisture controls photosynthesis and respiration, microbial activity, and organic matter decay.

*(Reference: p. 2)*

### 02. Root respiration
Drought cut its share from 33% to 16% (subtropical forest) and 66% to 35% (dry grassland).

*(Reference: p. 3)*

### 03. CO₂
'Peak and Decline' pattern, peaking near 40% WFPS.

*(Reference: pp. 4–5)*

### 04. CH₄ and N₂O
Peak at 60–80% and ~80% WFPS; site values vary (paddy CH₄ peak 99%).

*(Reference: pp. 4–5)*

### 05. Drought and floods
Drought limits uptake; floods create anaerobic conditions that favour methane.

*(Reference: pp. 4, 9)*

### 06. Rewetting
Drying-rewetting cycles raise CO₂ by 35.7%.

*(Reference: p. 5)*

### 07. AWD
Can cut rice CH₄ by up to 50% vs continuous flooding.

*(Reference: p. 7)*

### 08. Practices
Location-specific conservation agriculture, cover crops, mulching and agroforestry.

*(Reference: pp. 6–7)*

### 09. Policy
Shift from short-term yield to long-term carbon, with incentives and MRV.

*(Reference: p. 8)*
