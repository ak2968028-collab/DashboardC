## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. POC drives the gain
POC rose 21.8% while MAOC fell 5.3% (not significant).

*(Reference: p. 2)*

### 02. Less stable
The POC:MAOC ratio rose 29.1%.

*(Reference: p. 1)*

### 03. Two pools
POC is 27.4% and MAOC 70.0% of SOC on average.

*(Reference: p. 2)*

### 04. Forests and grazing
POC fell in planted forests and grazed grasslands.

*(Reference: pp. 2–3)*

### 05. Croplands
POC fell in Asian croplands and rose in North American ones.

*(Reference: p. 3)*

### 06. Protect POC
Less heavy grazing, conservation agriculture, forest protection.

*(Reference: pp. 1, 4)*

### 07. Stabilise it
Less disturbance, deep roots and N-rich inputs help POC become MAOC.

*(Reference: p. 5)*
