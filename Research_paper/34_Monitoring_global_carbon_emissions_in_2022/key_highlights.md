**Authors:** Zhu Liu (Tsinghua University), Zhu Deng (Alibaba Cloud), Steve Davis (University of California, Irvine), Philippe Ciais (LSCE, France; The Cyprus Institute)
**Journal / Year:** Nature Reviews Earth & Environment, vol. 4, pp. 205–206 (April 2023) (2023)
**Official DOI:** [https://doi.org/10.1038/s43017-023-00406-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43017-023-00406-z)

## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. New high
36.1 ± 0.3 GtCO₂ in 2022: +1.5% vs 2021, +7.9% vs 2020, +2.0% vs 2019.

*(Reference: p. 205)*

### 02. COVID dip was brief
−5.4% in 2020, +6.3% in 2021; no peak yet.

*(Reference: p. 205)*

### 03. Who emits
Power 39.3%, industry 28.9%, ground transport 17.9%, residential 9.9%.

*(Reference: p. 205)*

### 04. Aviation
International aviation +44% in 2022, still 25% below 2019.

*(Reference: p. 205)*

### 05. India
Emissions +7% in 2022 (7.9% above 2019); set to pass the EU as third largest emitter in 2023.

*(Reference: p. 205)*

### 06. China
First fall, −1.5%, but still 5.6% above 2019.

*(Reference: p. 205)*

### 07. Top five
23.3 GtCO₂, 65% of the global total.

*(Reference: p. 205)*

### 08. 1.5 °C countdown
IPCC budget: 283 GtCO₂ left, 7.1 years; newer estimate: 70 GtCO₂ left, 1.7 years.

*(Reference: pp. 205–206)*
