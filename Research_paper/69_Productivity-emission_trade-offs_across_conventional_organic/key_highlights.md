## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Farm survey
9 farms each under conventional, organic and natural farming; 2023–2024; Cool Farm Tool.

### 02. Rice
GHG −31.9% (organic) and −31.6% (natural); yield −18% and −8.99%.

### 03. Wheat
GHG −9.35% (organic) and −7.18% (natural); yield −16.38% and −40%.

### 04. Trade-off
Ratios 3.52 (rice, natural) and 0.18 (wheat, natural).
