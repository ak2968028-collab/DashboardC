## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. LULC mapping
Landsat 1991–2022, seven classes, random forest in Google Earth Engine; VCDR, 673 km².

### 02. Urban growth
Built-up 29.97 → 182.16 km² (about 6.1 times); gains of 37.4, 43.4 and 71.4 km² per period.

### 03. Farmland loss
Net −91.8 km² (72.9% → 59.3% of area); 114.8 km² of farmland became built-up.

### 04. Other classes
Barren −82%, fallow −26%, vegetation −22% (with a rise after 2011).

### 05. Strong agreement
Overall accuracy 86.8–92.8%; Kappa 0.846–0.916.
