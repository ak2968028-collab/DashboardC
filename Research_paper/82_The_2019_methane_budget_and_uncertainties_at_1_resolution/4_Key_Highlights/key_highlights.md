## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Sectors
Livestock 146.1 and rice 67.6 Tg CH₄/yr, both above the prior.

### 02. Fossil
82 ± 12 Tg, matching UNFCCC reports (80.4).

### 03. Total
39.5 Tg CH₄/yr vs 24.5 in the inventory.

### 04. Livestock
23.9 vs 13.0 Tg; signals overlap with rice and waste.
