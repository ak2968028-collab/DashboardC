## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Soil and water
Better structure, aeration and water retention; most useful in sandy or arid soils.

*(Reference: pp. 13, 16)*

### 02. Enzymes
Dehydrogenase, phosphatase and protease activity rise with biochar plus manure.

*(Reference: p. 13)*

### 03. Carbon
Stable carbon can remain for decades or centuries.

*(Reference: p. 14)*

### 04. Aggregates
More SOM and stable aggregates reduce erosion.

*(Reference: p. 14)*

### 05. Retention
Porosity and CEC hold N, P and K; fewer fertiliser applications needed.

*(Reference: p. 14)*

### 06. Runoff
Adsorbs ammonium, nitrate and phosphate, keeping them out of rivers.

*(Reference: p. 17)*

### 07. GHG
N₂O and CH₄ cuts are stated but need further investigation.

*(Reference: pp. 15, 19)*

### 08. Barriers
Cost, inconsistent quality, logistics; clay soils gain less.

*(Reference: pp. 18–19)*
