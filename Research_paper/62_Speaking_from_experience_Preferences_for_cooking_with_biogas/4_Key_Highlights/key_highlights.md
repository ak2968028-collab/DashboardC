## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Choice experiment
503 households, 8 districts of Odisha, Nov 2011 – Feb 2012; biogas and rocket stove vs chulha.

### 02. Smoke and fuel
Smoke reduction INR 4,785; fuel efficiency INR 3,074; maintenance INR 609 (not significant).

### 03. Biogas yes, rocket stove no
Positive premium for biogas at every experience level; negative premium (−INR 2,768) for the improved biomass stove.

### 04. Good vs bad experience
Biogas premium INR 7,348 (good), 5,744 (malfunction), 2,837 (none); values in 2011–12 rupees.

### 05. Risk and patience
Lower WTP among risk-averse and impatient households mainly where they lacked experience; the authors say the size of this effect is unclear.

### 06. Beyond subsidies
Technology trials, quality products, maintenance and customer service.
