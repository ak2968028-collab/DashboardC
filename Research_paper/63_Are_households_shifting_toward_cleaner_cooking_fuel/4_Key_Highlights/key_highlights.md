## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Three NFHS rounds
1,276,508 household records (71% rural); multinomial logit with wood as base.

### 02. Rural shift
Gas 11% → 39%, wood 68% → 48% (2005–06 to 2019–21).

### 03. Urban shift
Gas 63% → 86%; wood 17% → 8%.

### 04. Education
LPG use 11%, 17%, 36% (no education) vs 84%, 78%, 83% (higher education); rural higher-educated still ~20% wood.

### 05. Wealth
Richest >80% LPG; poorest about 10% LPG and >70% firewood.

### 06. Gender
Female-headed households are more likely to use LPG (regression), despite the abstract's wording.
