**Authors:** Sarah Duddigan, Liz J. Shaw, Tom Sizmur, Dharmendar Gogu, Zakir Hussain, Kiranmai Jirra, Hamika Kaliki, Rahul Sanka, Mohammad Sohail, Reshma Soma, Vijay Thallam, Haripriya Vattikuti, Chris D. Collins

| **28** | farms, 6 districts of Andhra Pradesh |

| **30–40 %** | groundnut and tomato gain |

## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. On-farm trials
ZBNF vs organic vs conventional, no tillage, 2019–2020; only 3 farms ran all 3 seasons.

### 02. Yield
ZBNF z-score 0.58 vs −0.34 organic and −0.24 conventional; significant only for groundnut and tomato.

### 03. Soil
Wetter, cooler soil and more earthworms under ZBNF; SOC unchanged.

### 04. Nutrients
Mostly no difference from conventional; less N in season 3.

### 05. Mulch confounded
Only ZBNF plots were mulched, so the driver cannot be isolated.
