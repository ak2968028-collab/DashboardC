## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Gap
Efficiency gap 0.17 (rice) and 0.23 (wheat); >40% of rice plots below average.

*(Reference: pp. 4–5)*

### 02. Drivers
Irrigation, improved seed and machinery.

*(Reference: p. 5)*

### 03. Intensity vs total
Efficiency cuts kg CO₂e per kg grain, not total per ha.

### 04. Smallholders
Often efficient and low-emitting.

*(Reference: p. 6)*
