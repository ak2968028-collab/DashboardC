# Key Highlights: Greenhouse gas emissions from global production and use of nitrogen synthetic fertilisers in agriculture

**Authors:** Stefano Menegat, Alicia Ledo, Reyes Tirado
**Journal / Year:** Scientific Reports, vol. 12, article 14490 (2022) (2022)
**Official DOI:** [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-18773-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-18773-w)

## Core Statistics & Results

| Metric | Finding / Significance |
| --- | --- |
| **1.13 Gt CO₂e** | N fertiliser chain, 2018 |
| **2.1%** | of global GHG emissions |
| **38.8%** | from manufacture |

## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Total
1,129 ± 171 Mt CO₂e in 2018: 10.6% of agricultural and 2.1% of global emissions.

*(Reference: pp. 1–2)*

### 02. Stages
Field 58.6%, manufacture 38.8%, transport 2.6%.

*(Reference: p. 2)*

### 03. Intensity
10.48 t CO₂e per t N on average; 148.1 kg CO₂e per person.

*(Reference: p. 3)*

### 04. Top emitters
China, India, USA and EU28 emit 62%.

*(Reference: p. 1)*

### 05. India
17.6 Mt N, 165.5 Mt CO₂e (14.7%), second after China (28.0%).

### 06. EF choice
Direct soil N₂O ranges 380–666 Mt CO₂e with the EF used.

*(Reference: p. 3)*

### 07. Spread
Total CV 15.1%; direct N₂O CV 42.2% drives it (urea CO₂ CV 45.5% but smaller).

*(Reference: pp. 4–5)*

### 08. Rising N₂O
Synthetic fertiliser N₂O rose 44% in 1990–2019 (FAO).

*(Reference: p. 6)*

### 09. 2050
260 Mt N/yr (intensive) or 85 Mt N/yr (higher NUE, within planetary boundary).

*(Reference: p. 6)*

### 10. Mitigation
Cut excess N, raise NUE, recycle manure N.

*(Reference: pp. 7–8)*
