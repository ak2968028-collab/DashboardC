# Key Highlights: Severe decline in large farmland trees in India over the past decade

**Authors:** Martin Brandt, Dimitri Gominski, Florian Reiner, Ankit Kariryaa, Venkanna Babu Guthula, Philippe Ciais, Xiaoye Tong, Wenmin Zhang, Dhanapal Govindarajulu, Daniel Ortiz-Gonzalo, Rasmus Fensholt
**Journal / Year:** Nature Sustainability, vol. 7, pp. 860–868 (July 2024) (2023)
**Official DOI:** [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41893-024-01356-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41893-024-01356-0)

## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Individual trees from space
RapidEye (5 m) and PlanetScope (3 m) with deep learning; about 0.6 billion trees in cropland, urban and bare land.

*(Reference: pp. 861–862)*

### 02. Density
0.6 trees/ha on average; up to 22/ha in Rajasthan and Chhattisgarh.

*(Reference: p. 862)*

### 03. Large trees vanishing
11 ± 2% of large trees (crown ~96 m²) gone in under a decade; 5.3 million more lost in 2018–2022 (±21%).

*(Reference: pp. 860, 862)*

### 04. Hotspots
Up to 50% of large trees and up to 22 trees/km² lost in parts of Telangana and Maharashtra.

*(Reference: p. 862, Fig. 1)*

### 05. Paddy and perception
New boreholes, paddy expansion, and the view that field trees reduce yields drove removals.

*(Reference: p. 862)*

### 06. Gross, not net
Plantations and new trees not counted; official net gains in trees outside forests can coexist.

*(Reference: p. 863)*
