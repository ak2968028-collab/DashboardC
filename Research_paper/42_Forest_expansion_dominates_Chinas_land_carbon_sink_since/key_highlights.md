**Authors:** Zhen Yu, Philippe Ciais, Shilong Piao, Richard A. Houghton, Chaoqun Lu, Hanqin Tian, Evgenios Agathokleous, Giri Raj Kattel, Stephen Sitch, Daniel Goll, Xu Yue, Anthony Walker, Pierre Friedlingstein, Atul K. Jain, Shirong Liu, Guoyi Zhou (16 authors)

## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. FAO cropland
Survey changes created false gains: +41 Mha reported vs −14 Mha real since 1980.

*(Reference: p. 2)*

### 02. Forest definition
FAO 'land use' definition understated new forests by 30 Mha (1990–2019).

*(Reference: p. 3)*

### 03. Sink
8.9 ± 0.8 Pg C gained 1980–2019, mostly in forest biomass.

*(Reference: p. 4)*

### 04. Forests
Natural forests 2.9 Pg C, planted 1.6 Pg C.

*(Reference: p. 6)*

### 05. Land-use change
~44% (abstract) or 50.3% (results); climate, N and CO₂ much less.

*(Reference: pp. 1, 5)*
