## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Ranking
Horti-silvipasture 29.94 > agri-horti-silviculture 22.56 > horti-agriculture 16.28 > boundary 15.67 t C/ha.

### 02. Biomass
66.55 t/ha in horti-silvipasture vs 34.87 in boundary plantation.

### 03. Sampling
6 tehsils → 6 blocks → 24 villages → 240 farmers.

*(Reference: p. 1)*

C = 0.45 × biomass; CO₂e = C × 3.67 (a stock, not a rate).
