## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Stability
Stable pool (97% of C) lasts ~556 years on average; labile pool (3%) 108 days.

*(Reference: p. 2386)*

### 02. Sink rule
A sink only if not used for energy and not burned or oxidised later; stores are soils, infrastructure, landfills.

*(Reference: pp. 2385–2386)*

### 03. Fertiliser carrier
Biochar-based N raised corn yield ~26% over urea in one tropical study.

*(Reference: p. 2395)*

### 04. Cattle methane
1% biochar in diets cut enteric methane 11–13% in vivo; with nitrate, 41% in one study.

*(Reference: pp. 2402–2403)*

### 05. Broiler ammonia
Biochar co-feed cut ammonia emissions by 17% in broiler chickens.

*(Reference: p. 2402)*

### 06. Biogas
Stabilises digesters and can raise methane yield.

*(Reference: pp. 2404–2409)*

### 07. Compost
Cut methane by up to 80% and lowered N₂O; CO₂ effects conflicting.

*(Reference: pp. 2419–2420)*

### 08. Kilns
Poorly built stoves and drum kilns can raise GHG and toxic emissions.

*(Reference: p. 2461)*

### 09. Policy and standards
Cost, low awareness and policy gaps; EBC and IBI standards.

*(Reference: pp. 2461–2462)*
