## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Yield
Happy Seeder full residue: +9.8–11.3% grain yield vs burning and removal.

### 02. Profit
Net return ₹43,160–57,080/ha; B:C 1.52–1.70.

### 03. SOC
Final 0.42% (T6) vs 0.32–0.33% with burning or removal.

### 04. Microbes
+47.9–60.4% at harvest over burning and removal.
