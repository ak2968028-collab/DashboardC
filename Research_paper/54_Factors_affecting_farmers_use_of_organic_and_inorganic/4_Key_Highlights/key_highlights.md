## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Where
Highest in Haryana, lowest in Nepal.

*(Reference: p. 51491)*

### 02. Who
Larger, wealthier, irrigated, more educated households.

### 03. Training
Fertiliser training cuts overuse; schooling does not.

*(Reference: p. 51491)*

### 04. Declining
47% of rice plots in Bihar and Nepal; falling everywhere.

*(Reference: p. 51484)*

### 05. Formula
Urea 46%, DAP 18%, manure 0.5% N.

*(Reference: p. 51483)*
