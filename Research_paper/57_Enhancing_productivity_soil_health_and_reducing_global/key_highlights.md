## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Seven scenarios
Farmers' rice-wheat practice (Sc1) vs six CA scenarios with zero tillage, residue retention and diversified rotations.

### 02. Residue kept
About 60–65% of maize stover and 25–30% of wheat stubble retained in Sc4–Sc5.

*(Reference: p. 3)*

### 03. More carbon
Surface organic C +63.7%, C stock +49.6%; rice-based CA gained most (+59.7%).

### 04. Better structure and nutrients
Aggregates +24.5%, mean weight diameter +118.7%; available N, P, K +10.2%, +28.6%, +21.8%.

*(Reference: p. 7)*

### 05. Soil quality index
+111% for all CA; +138% for maize-based rotations.

### 06. GWP almost halved
Net GWP fell 47.2% (CA mean), 55.6% for maize-based and 58.1% for legume-based rotations.

### 07. But N₂O rose
CA scenarios emitted 1428–2211 kg CO₂-eq/ha of N₂O vs 1041 in farmers' practice.

*(Reference: p. 10)*

### 08. Maize-based rotations best
System yield +20.7% and profit +40.3%; CA mean +8.5% yield and +23.0% profit.

### 09. Policy needed
Carbon credit payments and assured markets with supportive prices.
