## Important Findings & Key Takeaways

### 01. Sources
Fossil 9.9 ± 0.5 + land use 1.1 ± 0.7 = 10.9 ± 0.8 GtC (40.0 GtCO₂).

*(Reference: p. 4813)*

### 02. Sinks
Atmosphere 5.2, ocean 2.9, land 3.5 ± 0.9 GtC; imbalance −0.6 GtC.

*(Reference: pp. 4813–4814)*

### 03. India
+3.8%/yr over 2012–2021; 7% of world fossil CO₂ in 2021; 0.5 tC per person.

*(Reference: pp. 4826–4827)*

### 04. Cumulative fossil CO₂
1850–2021: USA 115 GtC (24%), EU 80 (17%), China 70 (14%); no India share.

*(Reference: p. 4826)*

### 05. Years left
From 2023: 105 GtC for 1.5 °C, 200 for 1.7 °C, 335 for 2 °C (≈ 9, 18, 30 years).

*(Reference: pp. 4814, 4847)*

### 06. Deforestation
1.8 ± 0.4 GtC/yr emitted; planting and forestry take up 0.9 ± 0.3 GtC/yr (half).

*(Reference: p. 4814)*

### 07. Three gaps
Land-use change emissions, northern extra-tropical land flux and the recent ocean sink trend.
